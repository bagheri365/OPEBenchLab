# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Research software release

- Stabilizes the synthetic ordered-slate OPE benchmark and estimator suite developed through M7.3.
- Includes M6 reproducibility and robustness protocols, M7 evidence ledgers, research draft, statistical reporting, and figure-generation CLIs.

Earlier milestone histories and experiment protocols are documented in `docs/`.
